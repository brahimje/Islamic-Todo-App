# Privacy Policy for Islamic Todo App

**Last Updated: January 26, 2026**

## Introduction

Islamic Todo ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Islamic Todo mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Tasks and Prayers**: Tasks, prayer completions, and spiritual activities you create and track
- **Settings and Preferences**: Your app preferences, prayer calculation methods, and notification settings
- **Backups**: Data you choose to export or backup

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Location Data**: We collect your device's location (with your permission) solely to calculate accurate Islamic prayer times for your area
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version, and app version for troubleshooting
- **Usage Data**: Anonymous usage statistics to improve app functionality

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Calculate accurate prayer times based on your location
- Send prayer reminders and task notifications
- Save your tasks, prayers, and spiritual progress
- Provide backup and restore functionality
- Improve app performance and user experience

## Data Storage

- **Local Storage**: All your data (tasks, prayers, settings) is stored locally on your device using encrypted databases
- **No Cloud Sync**: By default, we do not transmit your personal data to external servers
- **Backups**: When you create backups, they are stored on your device or your chosen cloud storage provider (you control where backups are saved)

## Data Sharing

We do **NOT**:
- Sell your personal information to third parties
- Share your prayer data, tasks, or personal information with advertisers
- Use your data for marketing purposes
- Transfer your data outside your device without your explicit action (backup/export)

We **MAY** share information only:
- With prayer time APIs (only location coordinates, no personal identifiers)
- When required by law or legal process
- To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property

## Third-Party Services

### Prayer Time Calculation
We use Islamic prayer time calculation libraries and may query prayer time APIs with only your location coordinates (no personal identifiers).

### Location Services
We use your device's location services only to determine accurate prayer times. You can deny location permission, but you'll need to manually set your location.

### Analytics (Optional)
If enabled, we collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app. This data is aggregated and contains no personally identifiable information.

## Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:
- **Access**: View all data stored in the app
- **Delete**: Delete all your data by uninstalling the app or using in-app data clearing
- **Export**: Export your data for backup or transfer purposes
- **Opt-Out**: Disable location services, notifications, or analytics at any time

## Children's Privacy

Islamic Todo is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us.

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:
- Local data encryption using Hive encrypted boxes
- No transmission of sensitive data over networks
- Secure coding practices to prevent unauthorized access

However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Posting the new privacy policy in the app
- Updating the "Last Updated" date
- For significant changes, displaying an in-app notification

## International Users

This app is available worldwide. Prayer time calculations adapt to your geographical location. Data storage is local to your device.

## Legal Compliance

### GDPR Compliance (European Users)
Under GDPR, you have rights to:
- Request access to your personal data
- Request correction of your personal data
- Request deletion of your personal data
- Object to processing of your personal data
- Request restriction of processing your personal data
- Request transfer of your personal data
- Withdraw consent

### CCPA Compliance (California Users)
California residents have the right to:
- Know what personal information is collected
- Know if personal information is sold or disclosed and to whom
- Say no to the sale of personal information
- Access their personal information
- Request deletion of personal information
- Not be discriminated against for exercising their privacy rights

**Note**: We do not sell personal information.

## Data Retention

- **Active Use**: Data is retained as long as the app is installed
- **After Uninstall**: All local data is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app
- **Backups**: Backup files you create are retained according to your chosen storage location's policies
